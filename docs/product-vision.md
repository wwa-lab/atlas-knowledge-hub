# Product Vision

Atlas Knowledge Hub helps teams convert scattered project documents into trusted, reviewable knowledge spaces.

## Experience

- Knowledge Space cards give each team or project a clear entry point.
- Folder and zip upload turns existing document packages into structured batches.
- Batch parsing converts documents through internal tools and produces traceable Markdown.
- Markdown Wiki pages become the durable, human-readable knowledge layer.
- Review queues let SMEs approve, reject, or request OCR fixes before publication.
- Knowledge graphs reveal concepts, dependencies, entities, and source relationships.
- Ask interfaces later provide source-grounded answers from approved knowledge only.

## Target Users

- SME: reviews parsed content, validates terminology, and approves publication.
- Developer: inspects source trace, system dependencies, and technical details.
- BA: organizes business concepts, process knowledge, and requirement references.
- Delivery lead: tracks batch progress, review status, and knowledge readiness.

## Product Principle

The product should make knowledge trustworthy before making it conversational. Reviewable Markdown with source trace is the foundation for graph and Ask features.
